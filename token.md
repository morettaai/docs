# The token

$MORETTA is the token of Moretta, on Solana. A holder of $MORETTA gets a tank that fills
with free credit for the chat every hour.

## Facts

| Fact | Value |
|---|---|
| Ticker | $MORETTA |
| Chain | Solana |
| Launchpad | pump.fun |
| Contract address | `5noESNFxmUYNcznHQEGjs9UQJ9LrL2aoFJp4HnSjpump` |

## Where to buy

$MORETTA trades on pump.fun: [pump.fun/coin/5noESNFxmUYNcznHQEGjs9UQJ9LrL2aoFJp4HnSjpump](https://pump.fun/coin/5noESNFxmUYNcznHQEGjs9UQJ9LrL2aoFJp4HnSjpump).

## What $MORETTA gives you

Hold at least $10 of $MORETTA, and your tank fills with $1 of credit every hour, up to
$24. The chat spends this credit. For the full rules, read [The tank](tank.md).

## Find the official contract address

Moretta publishes the contract address in these places only:

- The website: [moretta.ai](https://moretta.ai).
- The X account: [@morettadotai](https://x.com/morettadotai).
- These docs, on this page.

Before you buy, compare the contract address with these sources. A token with the same
name and a different address is not $MORETTA.

Moretta never asks for your seed phrase or your private key.
