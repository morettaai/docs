# FAQ

Short answers to common questions about Moretta. Each answer links to the page with the
full rules.

## What is Moretta?

Moretta is a private AI chat. You chat with the AI model of your choice, and your chat
history stays in your browser. Read [What is Moretta](what-is-moretta.md).

## How do I chat for free?

Hold at least $10 of $MORETTA. Your tank then fills with $1 of free credit every hour, up
to $24, and the chat spends it. Read [The tank](tank.md).

## Which models can I use?

The app lists hundreds of models from many providers, such as Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok,
and DeepSeek. You can switch the model in the middle of a chat.

## Is it really private?

Your chat history stays in your browser, and the Moretta server keeps no messages. A model
provider never sees your wallet. Read [Privacy](privacy.md).

## Which wallets work?

Solana wallets that support Wallet Standard work, such as Phantom, Solflare, and
Backpack.

## Does signing in cost anything?

No. The sign-in signature sends no transaction and costs nothing.

## What happens if I sell my $MORETTA?

Your tank stops filling when your holding falls below the line. The credit that is still
in your tank stays, and you can spend it in the chat.

## When does the app open?

Follow [@morettadotai](https://x.com/morettadotai) on X for the date.
