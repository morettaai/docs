# FAQ

Short answers to common questions about Moretta. Each answer links to the page with the
full rules.

## What is Moretta?

Moretta is a private AI chat. You chat with the AI model of your choice, and your chat
history stays in your browser. Read [What is Moretta](what-is-moretta.md).

## How do I chat for free?

Hold at least $10 of $MORETTA. Your tank then fills with $1 of free credit every hour, up
to $24, and the chat spends it. Read [The tank](tank.md).

## Which models can I use?

The app lists hundreds of models from many providers, such as Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok,
and DeepSeek. You can switch the model in the middle of a chat. The app also lists dozens
of image and video models, such as Nano Banana 2, Seedream, Veo, and Kling.

## Can I make images?

Yes. Turn on **Image** in the chat, pick an image model, and describe the image that you
want. Your tank pays for each image. Read [What is Moretta](what-is-moretta.md).

## Can I compare image models?

Yes. Turn on **Image** and then **Compare** in the chat, pick two to four image models, and
describe the image that you want. The images show up side by side, and your tank pays for
each image. Read [What is Moretta](what-is-moretta.md).

## Can I send my own images?

Yes. Attach up to 4 images to a prompt in the chat, in image mode, or in compare mode. A
video starts from one image that you attach. The Moretta server keeps no copy of your images.
Read [What is Moretta](what-is-moretta.md).

## Can I make videos?

Yes. Turn on **Video** in the chat, pick a video model, an aspect ratio, and a length, and
describe the video. A video takes about 30 seconds to a few minutes. Your tank pays for
the video when it starts. Read [What is Moretta](what-is-moretta.md).

## Can Moretta check a token for a rug?

Yes. Turn on **Research** in the chat and paste the contract address. An AI agent reads the
chain and writes a safety report with 11 signals, each green, yellow, or red. The report shows
risk signals, not a promise, so it never calls a token safe. Read
[Onchain research](onchain-research.md).

## Is it really private?

Your chat history, images, and videos stay in your browser. The Moretta server keeps no
messages, images, or videos. A model provider never sees your wallet. Read
[Privacy](privacy.md).

## Which wallets work?

Solana wallets that support Wallet Standard work, such as Phantom, Solflare, and
Backpack.

## Does signing in cost anything?

No. The sign-in signature sends no transaction and costs nothing.

## What happens if I sell my $MORETTA?

Your tank stops filling when your holding falls below the line. The credit that is still
in your tank stays, and you can spend it in the chat.

## Where do I chat?

Open [app.moretta.ai](https://app.moretta.ai) and connect a Solana wallet.
